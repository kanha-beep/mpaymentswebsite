Merchant underwriting essentials

Help Underwriters Understand How Your Business Operates

Acquirers and payment providers evaluate each merchant individually. They commonly assess your business model, processing history, expected volume, average transaction size, refunds, chargebacks, compliance exposure and supporting documentation.

01

Business Profile

Create a consistent view of your legal entity, product or service, fulfilment process, pricing and customer-facing policies.

02

Processing History

Organise previous processing statements, volumes, average ticket values, refund data and chargeback ratios where available.

03

Risk Controls

Show how fraud screening, clear descriptors, customer support, refunds and dispute handling reduce avoidable payment risk.

04

Provider Fit

Match your merchant category, markets and payment needs with providers that have appetite for your risk profile.

A more durable approval process

High-Risk Is a Category, Not a Verdict

A business may be classed as high-risk because of its industry, transaction pattern, delivery model, chargeback exposure or regulatory complexity. That classification calls for deeper underwriting and transparent operating practices—not exaggerated approval promises.

01

Prepare Before You Apply

Make sure your website, policies, product information, processing assumptions and documents tell the same operational story.

02

Discuss Terms Beyond the Rate

Review rolling reserves, settlement timing, volume caps, refund handling and chargeback-management expectations alongside pricing.

03

Maintain a Stable Processing Profile

Keep descriptions, fulfilment, support and dispute management aligned with the profile presented during merchant underwriting.
